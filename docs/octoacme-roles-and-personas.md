# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Security & Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security & Compliance Leads help ensure projects meet required security, privacy, and regulatory standards while preserving delivery speed and stakeholder trust.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical and product designs for security and compliance risks
- Coordinate vulnerability triage, remediation priorities, and control validation
- Review data handling, access controls, and privacy implications
- Partner with delivery leads to ensure requirements and release decisions align with policy and risk standards

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Enable safe, compliant delivery
- Increase trust across customers, stakeholders, and operational teams

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and architecture check-ins
- Risk triage with engineering and project leads
- Security review checkpoints during planning and release readiness

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to review implementation choices, secure architecture decisions, and remediation plans
- Partners with Product Managers to ensure requirements account for compliance, customer trust, and risk considerations
- Coordinates with Project Managers and Release Managers to align security checks, approval gates, and release timing

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide the technical strategy, design direction, and quality decisions needed to deliver the project effectively and sustainably.

### Responsibilities
- Define or validate system architecture, technical standards, and integration patterns
- Identify key dependencies, technical risks, and trade-offs early in delivery
- Support engineering decisions with design guidance and reusable patterns
- Help maintain cross-team consistency in implementation quality and maintainability

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and resilient solutions
- Reduce rework caused by unclear technical direction
- Balance speed, quality, and architectural health

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Dependency and sequencing planning with PMs and stakeholders
- Technical feedback during sprint planning and risk review sessions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Developers to align implementation decisions, quality expectations, and design trade-offs
- Provides technical input to Product Managers and Project Managers on feasibility, sequencing, and dependency planning
- Works with Security & Compliance Leads to validate secure architecture and risk mitigation decisions

---

## Release Manager / Deployment Coordinator

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness, deployment sequencing, and stakeholder communication around go-live activities.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm release readiness and rollback plans
- Coordinate deployment timing with project and operational stakeholders
- Validate post-deployment checks, communications, and follow-up actions
- Help reduce release risk through preparation, ownership, and clarity

### Goals
- Improve release predictability
- Minimize deployment disruption and rollback risk
- Ensure users and stakeholders are informed and prepared

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness meetings and deployment checklists
- Go-live updates and stakeholder notifications
- Post-deployment review and incident coordination when needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to align release windows with milestones and stakeholder dependencies
- Coordinates with Developers and Technical Leads to confirm deployment readiness and rollback scenarios
- Partners with Product Managers and Customer Success/Support roles to communicate user impact and release notes

---

## Customer Success / Support Partner

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support partners connect project delivery to customer impact, operational readiness, and feedback from real-world usage.

### Responsibilities
- Represent user experience and support needs in planning and release work
- Surface customer issues, usage patterns, and feedback into project priorities
- Help define support readiness, escalation paths, and launch communications
- Support adoption, feedback loops, and post-release follow-up

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention
- Reduce support friction and operational confusion
- Ensure delivery is informed by lived user experience

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews
- Launch support readiness and escalation planning
- Cross-functional updates tied to release and adoption outcomes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides input to Product Managers on customer pain points, feature requests, and prioritization
- Shares operational insights with Project Managers and Release Managers before and after launch
- Helps Developers and Technical Leads understand user-impacting edge cases, support scenarios, and customer expectations

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The expanded set of roles helps teams define ownership, escalation paths, and cross-functional collaboration more clearly across project lifecycles.
